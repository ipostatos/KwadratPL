Privacy policy

Short and honest: what data we keep, for how long, and how to delete it.

What data we store

  • On the server: your Telegram ID, chosen language, your alerts (city, rental type, filters) and quiet-hours settings. Needed to send you notifications.
  • On your device (localStorage): favourites and saved searches. We don't see them — they stay only with you.

How long we keep it

  • Alerts and account — until you delete them.
  • Night-notification buffer (quiet hours) — up to 3 days.
  • A technical marker of shown listings — up to 60 days.
  • Local data on your device — until you clear it.

AI listing breakdown

When you tap “✨ AI breakdown”, the listing text and your chosen language are sent to the provider Anthropic (Claude) for translation, a summary and a risk score. The answer is cached for up to a day.

We don't send your personal data to the AI and don't use your requests to train models.

What we do NOT collect

  • Phone number, e-mail or payment details.
  • Precise location.
  • The content of your Telegram chats.

Your rights

  • Delete your server data with the “Delete my data” button in the “Alerts” section. It also clears this device.
  • Turn off notifications anytime with the /off command in the bot chat.
  • Write to us about anything data-related.
